## In the exam

- **Read the last line first.** Many items describe a view, then ask one precise thing: the chart type, the next step, or why a number changed.
- **Count the answers.** In a multiple-select item, choose exactly as many as it asks for.
- **Read the shelves.** For "which steps create this chart?", rebuild the view in your head: what is on Columns, Rows and the Marks card, and what's blue or green (<LessonLink id="4.2" />).
- **Watch for near-miss options.** `.tds` vs. `.tdsx`, live vs. extract (<LessonLink id="1.1" />), combined vs. dual axis, group vs. alias: many wrong options are the right idea applied to the wrong case.
- **Don't spend too long on one item.** Pick your best answer and move on rather than lose minutes the later items need.
- **Superstore:** many items assume Tableau's Sample-Superstore data (Sales, Profit, Category › Sub-Category, Region, Order Date). The lessons' Superstore notes map it to Driftlight.
